How this is counted

From the farm's receiver. Every path it records, at any height, is checked near Dulles. A landing is a path that comes down below 1,500 ft within 5 nautical miles of the airport after being at 2,500 ft or higher in the 20 minutes before; a take-off is one below 1,500 ft there that climbs to 2,500 ft within 15 minutes. Each is counted in the quarter-hour it happens, Eastern time.

Averages. Each day of the week is the average of the days of that weekday in the period; "Every day" is the average of all of them. Only whole days are used.

How good is it? About 450 landings and 440 take-offs a day were found in the first four weeks, 91% with airline callsigns. The counts haven't been checked against official Dulles figures, so treat them as approximate; the busy times don't depend on the exact totals. Aircraft the receiver doesn't hear low near Dulles aren't counted.

Kept up to date by the Pi, which counts each hour once it's over.
